Ali Iranmanesh

I am an undergraduate at the Pennsylvania State University, Schreyer Honors College, studying computer science and graduating in 2027. I work on AI safety and security.

My research asks how embodied and multimodal AI systems break when the physical world stops cooperating: typographic attacks on household manipulation robots with Peng Liu at Penn State, and adversarial attacks on the vision side of vision language action models with Yuan Tian at UCLA. I also work on LLM safety evaluation with Liwei Jiang.

Research
