I also test their blind spots, looking at whether models recognize the real-world constraints that make their advice useless in tough situations.

HearSayBench: Can LLMs Navigate from Abstract Human Rights to Lived Lives?

Ali Iranmanesh*, Ava Iranmanesh*, Sobhan Lotfi*, Liwei Jiang

A benchmark probing whether models recognize the unstated structural constraints that make standard advice impossible for people in adversarial conditions. Across 11 frontier and open-weight models we find a systemic 37% drop between understanding a situation and giving advice that respects it. Oral at the Pluralistic Alignment Workshop, ICML 2026.

Not What You Asked For: Typographic Attacks in Household Robot Manipulation

Ali Iranmanesh, Peng Liu

An evaluation of typographic attacks against open-vocabulary household manipulation robots. We demonstrate how physical text perturbations can override a model's visual judgment and poison its persistent 3D semantic map, causing the robot to physically grasp and deliver the wrong object without touching the underlying model itself.
