NeurIPS 2026 · submitted

HearSayBench: Can LLMs Navigate from Abstract Human Rights to Lived Lives?

Ali Iranmanesh*, Ava Iranmanesh*, Sobhan Lotfi*, Liwei Jiang

A benchmark probing whether models recognize the unstated structural constraints that make standard advice impossible for people in adversarial conditions. Across eleven frontier and open-weight models we find a systemic gap between understanding a situation and giving advice that respects it. Oral at the Pluralistic Alignment Workshop, ICML 2026.

USENIX Security 2027 · submitting

Not What You Asked For: Typographic Attacks in Household Robot Manipulation

Ali Iranmanesh, Peng Liu

We show how physical-world text perturbations placed in a household scene can jailbreak vision-language manipulation robots — steering the binding stage that decides which object an instruction refers to, without touching the model itself.
