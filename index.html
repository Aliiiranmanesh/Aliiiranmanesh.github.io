HearSayBench: Can LLMs Navigate from Abstract Human Rights to Lived Lives?

Ali Iranmanesh*, Ava Iranmanesh*, Sobhan Lotfi*, Liwei Jiang

A benchmark probing whether models recognize the unstated structural constraints that make standard advice impossible for people in adversarial conditions. Across 14 frontier and open-weight models, we find a systemic 25% drop between understanding a situation and giving advice that respects it. Oral at NeurIPS 2026.

Not What You Asked For: Characterizing Typographic Attack Surfaces and Harm in Embodied Vision-Language Agents

Ali Iranmanesh, Peng Liu

A characterization of typographic attack surfaces and harm in open-vocabulary embodied vision-language agents. We demonstrate how physical text perturbations can override a model's visual judgment and poison its persistent 3D semantic map, propagating through planning and manipulation to make the robot physically grasp and deliver the wrong object without modifying the underlying model.
